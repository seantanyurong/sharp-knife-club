Every Mid-Autumn Festival, the same small tragedy plays out in Singaporean homes. A beautiful mooncake goes on the plate, someone cuts it into quarters, and the result is a crumbly crust, a dragged lotus paste, and a salted egg yolk smeared across the cut.

Mooncakes are expensive and are often shared with guests, so presentation matters. The good news is that clean slices are easy once you know the trick, and most of it comes down to the knife.

###### Why Mooncakes Are Hard to Cut

A traditional baked mooncake has three very different textures:

- **A thin, delicate crust** that cracks under pressure
- **A dense, sticky filling** such as lotus paste, red bean, or mixed nuts
- **A firm, oily salted egg yolk** in the centre

A dull knife has to push hard to get through the filling. That pressure crushes the crust and drags the yolk. Snow skin mooncakes are softer but even stickier, and they tear if the knife hesitates.

###### The Best Knife for Mooncakes

**A sharp, thin, smooth-edged knife.** A chef knife, santoku, or petty knife all work well. Thinner is better because there is less blade to drag through sticky filling.

**Avoid serrated knives.** It feels like a bread knife should work, but serrations tear the crust and chew up the paste.

**Avoid the plastic knife in the box.** It's for convenience, not clean cuts.

**For mixed nut (wu ren) mooncakes**, use a slightly sturdier knife, since the nuts and candied fruit resist more.

###### The Technique for Clean Mooncake Slices

1. **Chill it briefly.** For baked mooncakes, 15 to 20 minutes in the fridge firms up the filling. Snow skin mooncakes should already be cold.
2. **Warm and dry the blade.** Dip the knife in hot water, then wipe it dry. A warm blade glides through dense paste more easily.
3. **Cut straight down in one motion.** Press down firmly and evenly. Don't saw or rock.
4. **Slide the knife out cleanly.** Pull it straight back toward you rather than lifting it up through the cake.
5. **Wipe the blade between every cut.** This is the most important step. Paste and yolk on the blade will smear the next slice.
6. **Re-warm the blade if it starts sticking.**

For perfect quarters, make the first cut through the centre, rotate the plate 90 degrees, and cut again. For eighths, repeat on the diagonals.

###### Cutting Snow Skin Mooncakes

Snow skin is soft and sticky, so:

- Keep it cold until just before cutting
- Use a very sharp, thin knife
- Lightly dust the blade with a little cornstarch or wipe it with a damp cloth between cuts
- Cut decisively and avoid pausing halfway

###### Presenting Mooncakes to Guests

- Cut on a clean wooden or plastic board, then move slices to the serving plate with the flat of the knife
- Arrange quarters with the yolk side facing outward so it shows
- Serve with tea. The bitterness balances the sweetness of the filling.

###### Don't Cut on the Plate

It is tempting to cut mooncakes straight on the serving plate. Ceramic and porcelain are much harder than knife steel, and even a few cuts can dull a fine edge noticeably. Use a cutting board, then transfer.

###### Final Thoughts

A clean mooncake slice is one of those small details guests notice. Chill the cake, warm the blade, cut straight down, and wipe between every slice. Most importantly, use a knife that's actually sharp.

If your knife squashes a mooncake rather than cutting it, it's time for a sharpening before the festive season really gets going.
